Stage 6 goal:

Improve within-session fatigue management so the generator can softly avoid stacking exercises that heavily fatigue the same muscles or structures without a clear reason.

This must be a soft preference, not a hard prohibition.

Examples of undesirable stacking:

- Bench Press followed by another heavy press when chest, triceps, and anterior delts are already heavily loaded
- Romanian Deadlift followed by Good Morning
- Heavy Barbell Row followed by another highly fatiguing unsupported row
- Multiple demanding knee-dominant compounds in the same session without justification

The system must still allow these combinations when they provide a clear volume, target-resolution, priority, or exercise-selection advantage.
