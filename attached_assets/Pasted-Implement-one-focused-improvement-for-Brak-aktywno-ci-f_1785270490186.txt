PART 4 — SPLIT-AWARE COVERAGE
==================================================

For Full Body sessions:

- every session should contain a lower-body movement,
- every session should contain a push,
- every session should contain a pull,
- distribute knee-dominant and hip-dominant work across the week.

For Upper / Lower:

- every Upper session should contain at least one push and one pull,
- across Lower sessions include both knee-dominant and hip-dominant work.

For another split:

- ensure all five required movement patterns are represented across the week.

Do not add exercises beyond session-duration capacity just to satisfy a small
muscle target.

==================================================
PART 5 — BALANCING BEFORE ACCESSORIES
==================================================

Before selecting or preserving an isolation exercise for:

- calves,
- adductors,
- biceps,
- triceps,

check whether any major movement pattern is missing.

If a major pattern is missing, prioritise a legal compound or simple
multi-joint exercise for that pattern.

Examples of required behaviour:

- If lats and back pulling volume are zero, do not preserve a redundant arm
  isolation instead of selecting a pull.

- If quadriceps and glutes are already highly covered but hamstrings are very
  low, prefer a legal hip hinge or hamstring movement over another redundant
  knee-dominant exercise.

- If chest and triceps are covered but back is missing, prefer a pull over
  another push.

- Do not add a direct biceps exercise solely because biceps is below its target
  when pulling work is still missing.

- Do not add a direct triceps exercise solely because triceps is below its
  target when balanced pushing and pulling have not been established.

Use the existing effective-volume calculator when comparing candidate exercises
or swaps.
