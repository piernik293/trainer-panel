============================================================
2. STEP 5 UNLOCK RULES
============================================================

Unlock "Podgląd" only when the generator draft is structurally valid.

Required conditions:

- client profile step is valid,
- plan assumptions step is valid,
- weekly volume preview is valid,
- daily distribution preview is valid,
- Step 4 is currently available,
- every workout day contains at least one selected exercise,
- every selected exercise has a stable exerciseId,
- working sets are whole integers from 1 to 6,
- minimum repetitions are valid positive integers,
- maximum repetitions are valid positive integers,
- minimum repetitions do not exceed maximum repetitions,
- RIR is an integer from 0 to 5,
- rest time is a positive integer.

Do not block "Podgląd" because:

- planned volume is not fully covered,
- proposed volume is exceeded,
- a muscle has additional unplanned volume,
- an exercise creates indirect volume,
- an informational recommendation warning exists,
- an avoided exercise was consciously accepted by the trainer,
- a non-blocking metadata warning exists.

Volume warnings are advisory.

They must not prevent the trainer from reviewing the plan.

If Step 5 is locked, show:

"Uzupełnij wszystkie dni treningowe i popraw nieprawidłowe parametry ćwiczeń, aby przejść do podglądu."

Do not unlock Step 5 based only on the current tab number.

Use real generator-draft validity.
