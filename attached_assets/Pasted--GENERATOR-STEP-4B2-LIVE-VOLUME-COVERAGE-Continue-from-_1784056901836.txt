2. CALCULATION
============================================================

For every selected exercise use the existing stored muscle assignments and
coefficients.

effectiveContribution =
workingSets × storedCoefficient

Example for 3 sets:

- coefficient 1.00 → 3.00
- coefficient 0.50 → 1.50
- coefficient 0.25 → 0.75

Requirements:

- calculate on the backend,
- use all valid muscle assignments,
- do not use mainMuscleGroup as a replacement for assignments,
- do not assume coefficient 1.00 when data is missing,
- do not round before summing,
- round only displayed results to a maximum of two decimal places.

One exercise may contribute to several muscles.

Count every contribution once.
