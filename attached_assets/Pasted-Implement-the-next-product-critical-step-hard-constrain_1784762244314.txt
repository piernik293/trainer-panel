PART 1 — HARD CONSTRAINTS FOR EXCLUDED EXERCISES
====================================================

IMPORTANT:
Negative exercise preferences / exercises to avoid must NOT behave like weak preferences.
They must behave like hard constraints.

Required behavior:
- if the client excludes "martwy ciąg", the generator must block the entire deadlift/hinge family when appropriate:
  - martwy ciąg konwencjonalny
  - martwy ciąg sumo
  - martwy ciąg rumuński
  - kettlebell deadlift
  - and equivalent deadlift-family variants
- if the client excludes "wyciskanie nad głowę", the generator should block OHP-family variants
- if the client excludes another exercise, support either:
  - blocking the exact exercise only
  - or blocking the whole movement family
depending on the current available data/model

Implementation intent:
- prefer family-level blocking when the excluded exercise clearly implies a movement family
- do NOT let near-synonyms or variants slip through
- if a blocked family removes many exercises, the generator must choose the next best compatible family, not silently ignore the constraint

IMPORTANT:
Show this logic explicitly in the final plan explanation:
- what was excluded
- whether the exact exercise only or the whole movement family was blocked
- why

====================================================
PART 2 — PUSH-UP RULE (CONDITIONAL, NOT DEFAULT)
====================================================

Push-ups must NOT be treated as universally bad or universally good.
They must be context-dependent.

Push-ups should be favored when:
- training location = home
- training location = outdoor gym / plener
- equipment is limited
- the client explicitly prefers push-ups
- goal = sprawność / kondycja / bodyweight-oriented work / athletic bodyweight work
- client is beginner or no-activity and push-ups are used as an appropriate simple pattern

Push-ups should be deprioritized when:
- full gym is available
- experience = intermediate or advanced
- better pressing options exist for the selected goal
- goal = siła / budowa sylwetki / cel sportowy and gym equipment gives stronger alternatives
- the client did NOT prefer push-ups

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT ban push-ups.
Do NOT let them appear as a default main-ish upper press in profiles where better options clearly exist.

====================================================
PART 3 — COMPETITIVE ATHLETE WEEKLY STRUCTURE OVERRIDE
====================================================

This is a product rule, not just a scoring tweak.

For profiles such as:
- Goal = Cel sportowy
- athleteStatus = zawodowo
- sport priorities include Dynamika and/or Szybkość
- sufficient experience level
- enough weekly training days (for example 4)
- enough session duration (for example 90 min)
- gym access available

the generator must be allowed to override a generic weekly structure like:
- Upper / Lower / Upper / Lower

and propose a more athletic structure, for example:
- Upper / Lower / FBW athletic / Plyo-Speed
or
- Power Lower / Upper Strength / Plyo-Dynamic / FBW support

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT force this for every sport client.
Do it only when the profile justifies it.

The generator must also explain this clearly in the summary:
Example intent:
- "Zmieniono klasyczny układ Upper/Lower 2x na układ Upper/Lower/FBW/Plyo, ponieważ klient jest sportowcem zawodowym i wybrał priorytet dynamika/szybkość."

This explanation is required.
Do NOT silently change structure without telling the user why.

PART 4 — PLAN EXPLAINABILITY IN SUMMARY
====================================================

The current summary is too weak and does not build enough trust.

Add a compact but clear explainability block to the final plan summary.

Required sections:

A. "Dlaczego taki plan?"
Include short structured bullets such as:

D. "Uwagi generatora"
Short trust-building notes, for example:
- higher priority on back/shoulders/legs
- athletic emphasis due to sport profile
