Refine the exercise-variety requirement for repeated plan-draft generation.

Do not implement a simple random rotation.
Do not modify volume calculations, split rules, duration estimation, safety
filters, contraindications, equipment filtering or movement-balance rules.
Do not run a broad audit.
Do not use subagents.

GOAL

For the same client, repeated draft generation should explore as many legal
exercise alternatives as possible before reusing previously selected exercises.

This applies to automatically generated draft alternatives.
It must not automatically change exercises inside an already accepted training
plan.

==================================================
1. SELECTION PRIORITY
==================================================

After applying all existing mandatory filters:

- client level,
- equipment,
- contraindications,
- trainer restrictions,
- movement pattern,
- required muscle coverage,
- session duration,
- safety rules,

rank legal candidates using this novelty order:

1. exercises never previously used for this client,
2. exercises used the fewest total times,
3. exercises used least recently,
4. existing exercise-quality score as the final tie-breaker.

Do not use pure randomness as the main selection rule.

Use stable exercise IDs.

==================================================
2. EXHAUST THE LEGAL POOL BEFORE REPEATING
==================================================

For a required movement pattern, do not reuse an exercise while another legal,
suitable and not-yet-used exercise exists.

Example legal pull pool:

A, B, C, D, E, F

Sequential generations should prefer:

Generation 1 → A
Generation 2 → B
Generation 3 → C
Generation 4 → D
Generation 5 → E
Generation 6 → F

Only after the suitable pool is exhausted may the generator return to A or
another previously used exercise.

Do not require the exact alphabetical order. The existing exercise-quality score
may determine the order among equally novel candidates.

==================================================
3. RECENT-GENERATION COOLDOWN
==================================================

An automatically selected exercise used in generation N should normally be
excluded from:

- N+1
- N+2
- N+3
- N+4

However, the main rule is stronger:

Even after the four-generation cooldown expires, prefer unused or less-used
legal exercises four-generation cooldown expires, prefer unused or less-used
legal exercises before returning to it.

Therefore, generation N+5 may reuse the exercise, but only when there is no
better unused or less-used suitable alternative.

==================================================
4. FREQUENCY AND RECENCY
==================================================

Persist per-client exercise usage containing at least:

- exerciseId,
- successful generation identifier,
- generation timestamp or sequence,
- total automatic-use count,
- most recent successful generation in which it appeared.

History must be isolated per client.

Only successful completed generations count.

Do not record failed, cancelled or invalid generation attempts.

==================================================
5. SAME-WEEK DIVERSITY
==================================================

Within one generated weekly plan, prefer different exercise variations across
days when legal alternatives exist.

Example for two Full Body sessions:

Day 1 push:
- knee push-up

Day 2 push:
- incline push-up

Prefer this over selecting the exact same exercise twice.

Do not introduce unnecessary variation when the split intentionally repeats a
main lift or when the trainer locked the exercise.

==================================================
6. MOVEMENT BALANCE HAS HIGHER PRIORITY
==================================================

Novelty must never override:

- required push/pull balance,
- knee-dominant and hip-dominant coverage,
- core/trunk coverage,
- contraindications,
- available equipment,
- trainer restrictions,
- session duration,
- safety.

A recently used legal pull is better than selecting no pull at all.

A safe repeated exercise is better than an unsafe new exercise.

For Brak aktywności fizycznej and Początkujący, preserve the current
general-development movement requirements.

==================================================
7. LOCKED AND MANUAL EXERCISES
==================================================

Manual or locked trainer selections:

- may repeat,
- must not be automatically replaced for novelty,
- should not be treated as generator selection failures.

Track automatic usage separately where possible.

==================================================
8. SMALL LEGAL POOL FALLBACK
==================================================

When all legal candidates for a required slot are inside the cooldown:

1. do not relax safety or restrictions,
2. choose the legal exercise used least recently,
3. when recency is equal, choose the exercise used the fewest times,
4. preserve movement balance,
5. record an internal reason:
   “powtórzenie wymagane z powodu ograniczonej puli legalnych ćwiczeń”.

==================================================
9. REQUIRED VERIFICATION
==================================================

Create a deterministic fixture with six legal exercises for one movement
pattern.

Verify:

- the first six successful generations use all six alternatives before repeating,
- the seventh generation selects according to least-used and least-recently-used
  ranking,
- no exercise repeats inside the previous four generations while an alternative
  exists,
- history is isolated between clients,
- failed generations do not update usage history,
- manual/locked exercises remain preserved,
- a small exercise pool falls back to the oldest legal exercise,
- contraindicated and unavailable-equipment exercises never become eligible,
- movement balance remains more important than novelty,
- the same exercise is not selected twice in one week when a suitable alternative
  exists.

Maximum 8 additional actions.
