# 10. Compact interview summaries and expandable details

The current client-profile cards look too much like long tables and require the trainer to scan many rows before seeing the important conclusion.

Improve only the presentation of the interview summary sections. Do not redesign the entire page or navigation.

## Regeneration and lifestyle

Replace the permanently expanded table with a compact summary card.

Default collapsed state:

Title:
`Regeneracja i styl życia`

Primary summary:
`Jakość regeneracji: Słaba`

Show the quality as a clearly visible status badge:

- Dobra
- Umiarkowana
- Słaba

Add an action:

`Pokaż szczegóły`

When expanded, show the source values:

- Aktywność codzienna: Siedzący
- Praca fizyczna: Nie
- Sen: 5 h
- Poziom stresu: 2/10
- Jakość regeneracji: Słaba

Allow the trainer to collapse it again using:

`Ukryj szczegóły`

Do not remove the underlying interview data. Only change its information hierarchy.

## Visual hierarchy

Use a “summary first, details on demand” pattern:

- important conclusion visible immediately,
- secondary interview values hidden until expanded,
- clear section title and icon,
- softer border and shadow,
- more internal spacing,
- avoid a dense spreadsheet-like appearance,
- preserve high readability and contrast.

Do not place every value in a separate heavy table row.

Prefer:

- compact summary header,
- status badges,
- small grouped detail blocks,
- consistent spacing,
- subtle separators only when needed.

## Apply consistently

Where appropriate, use the same compact pattern for other long profile sections:

- show the most important value or conclusion in the collapsed header,
- keep complete source data available after expansion,
- do not hide safety-critical information such as pain warnings or medical restrictions,
- pain and limitation warnings must remain visible without requiring expansion.

Do not collapse:

- pain warnings,
- medical contraindications,
- blocking generation errors,
- trainer decisions that still require action.

## Interaction and accessibility

- the whole header or a clear button may toggle expansion,
- display a chevron indicating the current state,
- support keyboard interaction,
- use `aria-expanded`,
- preserve logical reading order,
- do not rely only on color,
- remember the expanded state only during the current page session if needed.

## Mobile

On mobile:

- use full-width cards,
- prevent horizontal scrolling,
- place detail values in a one-column layout,
- keep tap targets comfortable.

## Focused tests

Add tests proving:

1. `Jakość regeneracji: Słaba` is visible while the section is collapsed.
2. Activity, physical work, sleep and stress are hidden initially.
3. Clicking `Pokaż szczegóły` reveals all source values.
4. Clicking `Ukryj szczegóły` collapses the section.
5. Pain and medical warnings are never hidden by this accordion.
6. The section exposes the correct accessible expanded state.
7. No interview data is removed or changed.
