==================================================

Persistent history only contains previous successful generations.

the automatically selected exercise IDs from earlier days in the current

- send currentGenerationUsedExerciseIds to generate-day

exists.

Safety, equipment, contraindications and required movement coverage remain
higher priority than same-week variety.

Do not write these current-generation IDs to persistent history before the
complete draft succeeds.

==================================================

==================================================

UNIQUE (client_id, generation_id, exercise_id)
