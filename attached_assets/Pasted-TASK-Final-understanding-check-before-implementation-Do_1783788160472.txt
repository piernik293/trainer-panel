6. EFFECTIVE VOLUME FORMULA
==================================================

For direct targets:

effective volume =
working sets × approved muscle coefficient

Weekly volume =
sum across every workout day

Warm-up sets do not count.

Do not round before validation.

Example:

Podciąganie na drążku — 3 working sets

Only approved targets from this feature are calculated:

- Mięsień najszerszy grzbietu, coefficient 1.0 → 3.0
- Mięsień czworoboczny grzbietu, coefficient 0.25 → 0.75
- Mięsień dwugłowy ramienia, coefficient 0.5 → 1.5

Do not calculate Mięsień obły większy in this feature.

==================================================

For one exercise and one approved group target:

target contribution =
working sets × highest coefficient among assigned muscles belonging to that target

Do not sum several coefficients from the same exercise inside the same group.

Example:

- anterior deltoid coefficient 1.0
- lateral deltoid coefficient 0.5

Mięśnie naramienne volume:

3 × max(1.0, 0.5) = 3.0

Not 4.5.
