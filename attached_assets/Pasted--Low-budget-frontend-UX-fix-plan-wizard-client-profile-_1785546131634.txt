## Low-budget frontend UX fix — plan wizard client profile step

Make only a small visual change to the existing “Profil klienta” step.

Do not modify backend, database, generator logic, validation, restrictions logic, routing or authentication.

Do not redesign the entire page.
Do not run the full test suite.
Do not read unrelated files.

### 1. Health warning

The yellow “Uwaga: zgłoszono zdrowie / ograniczenia” panel currently follows the trainer while scrolling.

Remove its sticky/fixed behavior.

It should:
- remain in its normal position at the top of the right column,
- scroll away naturally with the page,
- keep its current warning content and behavior,
- remain clearly visible without dominating the screen.

Remove classes or styles such as:
- sticky,
- fixed,
- top-*,
- position: sticky.

### 2. Reduce the number of visually separate white cards

The left column currently looks overcrowded because every interview section appears as a separate elevated white card.

Keep the existing content and section order, but visually simplify it:

- use one main white container for the client interview summary,
- make the internal sections flatter,
- separate sections with subtle dividers and spacing,
- remove strong shadows and excessive rounded borders from each internal section,
- retain section headings and icons,
- do not hide any information.

The result should feel like:

one interview summary panel
→ several clearly separated sections inside it

rather than many independent floating cards.

### 3. Preserve behavior

Do not change:
- displayed values,
- “wywiad” badges,
- adaptive-start status,
- health warning logic,
- navigation between wizard steps.

Verify only:
1. the yellow warning no longer follows scrolling,
2. all interview sections remain visible,
3. the page is less visually crowded,
4. desktop layout remains two-column,
5. mobile layout does not overflow.

Stop immediately after this visual change.

Report only:
- file changed,
- sticky style removed,
- visual card simplification applied.
