## 7. Trainer decision for the adaptive-start recommendation

For clients classified as:

- Brak aktywności fizycznej
- Początkujący

show a visible recommendation on the training-interview detail page:

“Rekomendowany start adaptacyjny”

Explain it in plain Polish:

“Start adaptacyjny skupia się na nauce podstawowych wzorców ruchowych, kontroli techniki oraz prostych i stabilnych ćwiczeniach z masą ciała lub lekkim oporem. Obciążenie jest wprowadzane stopniowo.”

Do not describe the adaptive start as always requiring zero external load. A light dumbbell, resistance band, cable, machine, or supported variation may be appropriate when it improves control and safety.

### Trainer decision

The system may recommend the adaptive start, but the trainer must make the final decision.

Provide two clear actions:

- “Zastosuj start adaptacyjny”
- “Pomiń i przygotuj standardowy plan”

Do not silently activate the adaptive-start generator mode.

Persist the trainer’s decision with states equivalent to:

- pending
- accepted
- declined

The client-list status and interview-detail page must show the current state:

- “Start adaptacyjny · Oczekuje na decyzję”
- “Start adaptacyjny · Zastosowany”
- “Start adaptacyjny · Pominięty”

### Generator behavior

When accepted:

- prioritize simple, stable and easy-to-learn movement variations,
- prioritize technique, control and repeatability,
- use bodyweight or light external resistance where appropriate,
- preserve the existing suitableForNoActivity safety requirements,
- avoid unnecessary complexity, instability, high-impact plyometrics and advanced variations,
- introduce progressive overload gradually,
- do not automatically fill the plan only with unloaded exercises.

When declined:

- use the normal generator appropriate to the client’s declared level,
- continue to respect all pain, medical, equipment and movement restrictions.

Pain-related restrictions remain separate from the adaptive-start decision. A localized complaint must not automatically regress the entire plan.

### Required focused tests

Add tests proving:

1. No-activity clients receive the adaptive-start recommendation.
2. Beginner clients receive the recommendation.
3. Intermediate and advanced clients do not receive it automatically.
4. The recommendation explains what adaptive start means.
5. The trainer can accept or decline it.
6. No adaptive-start mode is activated before the trainer decides.
7. The decision is persisted and visible on the client list and interview detail.
8. Accepted mode uses simple and stable exercises with gradual loading.
9. Declining the recommendation preserves the normal generator.
10. Pain warnings do not automatically activate adaptive start for the whole plan.
