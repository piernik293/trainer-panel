FEATURE 1 — PAIN DETECTION / RED ALERT
==================================================

If the client writes words suggesting pain in HEALTH-RELATED free-text fields,
show a very clear RED warning prominently on the trainer planning screen.

IMPORTANT SCOPE RULE:
Pain keyword detection must run ONLY in health / limitation / condition description fields.
Do NOT scan unrelated survey fields such as:
- training goal
- split preference
- general notes unrelated to health
- other non-medical preference fields

Pain detection must be:
- case-insensitive
- accent-insensitive

Minimum requirement:
- "ból"
- "bol"

These must be treated as equivalent.
Example:
- "bol barku"
- "ból barku"
must trigger the same warning logic.

Relevant fields for pain detection should include only health-related free text, for example:
- "Opisz zdrowie i ograniczenia"
- health notes / limitations notes
- any survey textarea explicitly meant for health, injury, pain, or limitations

When pain is detected:
- show a strong RED alert banner
- the banner must be clearly visible near the top of the relevant trainer-facing planning screen(s)

Banner title example:
"Zwróć uwagę: klient zgłasza ból"

Banner content must clearly say:
- Zwróć uwagę na ćwiczenia, które mogą pogłębić ból
- Zwróć uwagę na ćwiczenia, które mogą poprawić samopoczucie lub zmniejszyć dolegliwości, jeśli są dobrze tolerowane
- W przypadku utrzymującego się bólu skieruj klienta do fizjoterapeuty

Important:
- do NOT diagnose
- do NOT pretend to know which specific exercise is medically correct automatically
- this is a trainer warning only

FEATURE 3 — MANDATORY CARDIO / STEPS RULE
==================================================

Add a planning rule for:
- sedentary lifestyle
OR
- fat-loss goal / reduction of body fat

If either condition is true:
- the system must add a mandatory non-strength activity recommendation

This should be implemented as:
EITHER
- a cardio day
OR
- a step target / walking prescription

Use sensible planning logic:

1. If the person is beginner / no-activity and declares more days than recommended for strength training:
- keep maximum 3 strength days
- convert extra days into cardio / activity days
- do NOT turn all extra days into more strength sessions

2. If there is no extra day available:
- add a mandatory step target instead

3. If the trigger is fat-loss goal or sedentary lifestyle:
- the recommendation must appear in the plan summary / assumptions
- it must not be optional or hidden

Examples:
- sedentary + 4 or 5 declared days + beginner -> 3 strength days max + extra cardio / activity day(s)
- sedentary + fat loss + 3 days -> keep strength split, add mandatory daily steps
- fat loss goal even without sedentary lifestyle may still trigger steps/cardio support

Required output in plan assumptions / summary:
- clearly show the cardio / activity recommendation
- examples:
  - "Dodatkowa aktywność: 1 dzień cardio tygodniowo"
  - "Cel aktywności: 8000–10000 kroków dziennie"
  - "Ze względu na siedzący tryb życia dodano obowiązkową aktywność tlenową"

Do NOT make this optional when sedentary lifestyle or fat-loss trigger is present.
It should be a required recommendation in the plan output.

FEATURE 4 — CONDITIONING GOAL RULE
==================================================

If the client chooses a training goal related to:
- "Poprawa kondycji"

then the plan must explicitly reflect that.

Rule:
- regardless of selected training frequency, the plan must include at least 1 Full Body strength session for general physical development
- and must prioritize cardio / conditioning work, because conditioning cannot be improved from strength training alone

This means:
- the plan must not become only strength-based
- cardio / conditioning must be clearly present as a required part of the plan

Suggested planning logic:
1. 2 training days:
- 1 FBW
- 1 cardio / conditioning day

2. 3 training days:
- minimum 1 FBW
- at least 1 cardio / conditioning day
- remaining day may be FBW or conditioning depending on recovery and level

3. 4+ declared days:
- keep strength simple
- prioritize cardio / conditioning in the additional days
- do NOT over-specialize the strength split

Required summary text example:
- "Cel poprawy kondycji → dodano obowiązkową jednostkę cardio oraz minimum jedną sesję FBW dla rozwoju ogólnego"
