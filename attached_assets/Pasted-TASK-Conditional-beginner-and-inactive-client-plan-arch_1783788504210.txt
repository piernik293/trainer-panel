==================================================
1. PURPOSE
==================================================

Prepare a separate workout-plan generation approach for clients whose training interview experience level is:

- Brak aktywności fizycznej
- Początkujący

The goal is to create simple, understandable and manageable plans built mainly around fundamental movement patterns.

Do not treat these two levels identically.

==================================================
2. FUNDAMENTAL MOVEMENT PATTERNS
==================================================

The plan should normally cover the following movement patterns where appropriate:

- squat pattern
- hip-hinge pattern
- horizontal push
- horizontal pull
- vertical push
- vertical pull
- simple unilateral lower-body movement
- trunk stability
- optional loaded carry when suitable

Do not require every pattern in every workout.

Distribute the relevant patterns across the complete training week.

==================================================
3. EXERCISE PROGRESSION LEVELS
==================================================

Do not automatically select advanced or technically demanding exercise versions.

For every fundamental pattern, support a progression from simpler to more demanding variations.

Examples:

Squat pattern:
- bodyweight squat to a box or bench
- goblet squat
- machine squat or leg press where appropriate
- barbell squat only when suitable

Hip hinge:
- hip-hinge learning drill
- kettlebell deadlift from an elevated position
- Romanian deadlift with dumbbells
- barbell deadlift only when suitable

Horizontal push:
- elevated push-up
- machine chest press
- dumbbell bench press
- barbell bench press when suitable

Horizontal pull:
- resistance-band row
- cable row
- supported dumbbell row
- more demanding free-weight row when suitable

Vertical pull:
- pulldown
- assisted pull-up
- pull-up only when suitable

Vertical push:
- machine shoulder press
- seated dumbbell press
- standing overhead press only when suitable

The system must choose the exercise variation, not merely the general exercise name.

==================================================
4. BRAK AKTYWNOŚCI FIZYCZNEJ
==================================================

For clients with:

Brak aktywności fizycznej

Use:

- beginner absolute volume ceilings
- clearly lower volume than a regular beginner plan
- fewer exercises
- fewer working sets
- simple exercise selection
- stable and supported variations where useful
- conservative progression
- longer adaptation period
- no advanced intensity techniques

Prioritise:

- learning movement patterns
- regularity
- control
- basic range of motion
- manageable fatigue
- confidence with exercise

Do not automatically use:

- heavy barbell deadlifts
- heavy barbell squats
- sets to failure
- forced repetitions
- drop sets
- rest-pause
- complex supersets
- technically advanced Olympic-lifting movements

==================================================
5. POCZĄTKUJĄCY
==================================================

For clients with:

Początkujący

Use the approved beginner volume guidelines.

Prefer:

- fundamental multi-joint exercises
- simple accessory exercises
- repeatable weekly structure
- enough exercise repetition to improve technique
- uncomplicated progression
- mostly moderate repetition ranges
- appropriate machine, dumbbell, bodyweight or barbell variations

A beginner may receive barbell squat, bench press or deadlift variations only when the exercise is appropriate according to:

- available equipment
- training location
- experience description
- existing exercise preferences
- trainer decision
- health information shown as context

Do not assume that every beginner should perform the barbell version.

==================================================
6. CLIENT INTERVIEW DATA
==================================================

Use the selected survey snapshot connected to the workout plan.

Consider:

- experience level
- training goal
- number of available training days
- available session duration
- training location
- available equipment
- daily activity
- exercise preferences
- exercises the client wants to avoid
- priority muscle groups
- health and injury answers as trainer-visible context

Do not diagnose medical conditions.

Do not automatically mark an exercise as medically safe or unsafe.

==================================================
7. VOLUME COMPATIBILITY
==================================================

Reuse the approved shared weekly-volume calculation service.

Do not create a separate volume formula for beginners.

All exercise selections and working sets must remain compatible with:

- the 11 approved volume targets
- experience-level guideline rows
- primary and secondary muscle coefficients
- weekly preferred ceilings
- weekly absolute ceilings
- per-session warnings

For “Brak aktywności fizycznej”:

- use beginner absolute ceilings
- remain clearly below preferred ceilings
- do not invent a fixed percentage reduction

For “Początkujący”:

- normally remain at or below beginner preferred ceilings
- do not treat the preferred ceiling as a mandatory minimum

==================================================
8. PLAN STRUCTURE
==================================================

Prefer simple structures based on realistic training frequency.

Examples:

2 days per week:
- Full Body A
- Full Body B

3 days per week:
- Full Body A
- Full Body B
- Full Body A/B rotation or three simple full-body sessions

Do not automatically create complicated bodybuilding splits for inactive or beginner clients.

Avoid excessive exercise variety.

Fundamental exercises may repeat across the week when this helps motor learning and progression.

==================================================
9. PROGRESSION FOUNDATION
==================================================

Prepare plan fields and logic for simple future progression.

Recommended progression concept:

- first improve technique and complete the repetition range
- then increase repetitions
- then increase load by a small step
- keep an appropriate number of repetitions in reserve
- reduce load or volume when the target cannot be performed with acceptable technique

Do not implement an advanced automatic progression engine unless it already exists.

For this task, prepare the architecture and trainer-facing guidance.

==================================================
10. SAFETY RULES
==================================================

Do not:

- select exercises only because they are multi-joint
- force barbell movements
- use advanced intensity methods
- train every set to failure
- ignore exercises the client wants to avoid
- exceed approved weekly absolute volume ceilings
- exceed the client’s available session duration
- create a plan with exercises lacking muscle assignments
- invent medical restrictions

The trainer keeps the final decision.

==================================================
11. REQUIRED RESULT
==================================================

Implement or prepare:

- separate rules for “Brak aktywności fizycznej”
- separate rules for “Początkujący”
- exercise difficulty and variation filtering
- fundamental movement-pattern coverage
- compatibility with the existing volume system
- trainer-visible explanation of why each exercise variation was selected
- warnings when an exercise is unusually difficult for the selected level

Do not automatically activate generated plans.

All generated plans must start as drafts and require trainer review.

==================================================

11. Existing plans and volume calculations still work.
12. No advanced intensity techniques are assigned to these levels.
