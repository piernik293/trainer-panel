# WORKOUT PLAN GENERATOR — CORRECTION BEFORE EXERCISE SELECTION
## Integer sets, priority bonuses, split compatibility and trainer guidance

Continue from the current Phase 3B repository state.

Do not implement the full exercise-selection engine yet.
Do not create or save workout plans, workout days, exercises or prescriptions.
Do not modify existing client, survey or workout-plan records.
Do not redesign unrelated pages.
Do not use AI.
Do not start the next phase automatically.

This is a focused correction of the generator rules before exercise selection.

============================================================
1. INTEGER WORKING SETS ONLY
============================================================

Planned working sets must always be whole integers.

This applies to:

- Phase 2 weekly selected volume,
- trainer volume overrides,
- Phase 3 daily allocations,
- values displayed in the wizard.

Allowed examples:

2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7...

Forbidden examples:

1.5, 2.5, 3.5, 13.5...

Change volume inputs and steppers from 0.5 increments to 1-set increments.

Automatic weekly recommendations must be rounded deterministically to a
whole integer while remaining inside the allowed thresholds.

Trainer overrides must reject non-integer values with:

VOLUME_OVERRIDE_NOT_INTEGER

Do not silently round trainer-entered values.

Internal effective-volume analysis may still use decimal contribution
coefficients, but a planned number of sets to perform must never be
fractional.

============================================================
2. DAILY DISTRIBUTION QUALITY
============================================================

Daily allocations must be whole integers.

For a positive target allocation:

- preferred: 3–4 sets,
- minimum: 2 sets,
- never create 1 set when an alternative valid distribution exists.

Prefer fewer meaningful exposures instead of distributing a target across
every eligible day.

Examples:

3 → [3]
4 → [4]
5 → [3, 2]
6 → [3, 3]
7 → [4, 3]
8 → [4, 4]
9 → [3, 3, 3]
10 → [4, 3, 3]
11 → [4, 4, 3]
12 → [4, 4, 4]

The exact weekly total must remain unchanged.

If selected weekly volume equals 1:

- allocate [1] on one day,
- emit TARGET_WEEKLY_VOLUME_TOO_LOW,
- do not split it.

Use integer arithmetic and deterministic tie-breaking.

============================================================
3. FIXED PRIORITY VOLUME BONUS
============================================================

Replace percentage-based priority-volume adjustments with fixed whole-set
bonuses.

Every selected priority group receives:

Large muscle group:
- +3 weekly working sets

Smaller muscle group:
- +2 weekly working sets

Large groups:

- chest,
- back,
- quadriceps,
- hamstrings,
- glutes.

Smaller groups:

- shoulders,
- biceps,
- triceps,
- calves,
- adductors,
- core / lower-back auxiliary targets.

Inspect the real 11 target IDs and broadCategory values and create one
centralized stable mapping.

The bonus is applied per priority body part / broadCategory, not separately
to every target belonging to that broad category.

Example:

If "Plecy" maps to more than one internal target, the total added bonus for
the entire priority remains +3, not +3 for every mapped target.

Distribute a shared-category bonus deterministically across its targets.

P1, P2 and P3 all receive the body-part bonus.
Priority order remains available for:

- earlier-week placement,
- later exercise selection,
- display order.

Automatic recommendation:

base recommendation + fixed priority bonus

It may exceed preferredMax when caused by a priority, but must never exceed
absoluteMax.

If above preferredMax:

PRIORITY_VOLUME_ABOVE_PREFERRED

If the complete bonus cannot fit below absoluteMax:

- cap only the automatic recommendation at absoluteMax,
- emit PRIORITY_BONUS_LIMITED_BY_ABSOLUTE_MAX.

Do not silently modify trainer overrides.

============================================================
4. SPLIT COMPATIBILITY WITH TRAINING DAYS
============================================================

Add one centralized compatibility map:

- full_body: minimum 1 day
- full_body_ab: minimum 2 days
- upper_lower: minimum 2 days
- upper_lower_full_body: minimum 3 days
- push_pull_legs: minimum 3 days
- push_pull_lower_full_body: minimum 4 days
- specialization: minimum 2 days
- custom: minimum 1 day

Maximum remains 6 days.

In the split-selection UI:

- incompatible split cards must be disabled,
- show: "Wymaga minimum X dni",
- do not allow selection,
- if the currently selected split becomes incompatible after changing
  trainingDays, clear it or replace it with the backend recommendation and
  show a clear message.

Do not display the current training-day count as if every split were valid.

Show separately:

- recommended,
- available,
- unavailable.

The backend must also reject an incompatible selected split using:

SPLIT_INCOMPATIBLE_WITH_TRAINING_DAYS

Do not rely only on frontend disabling.

============================================================
5. RECOVERY AND LIFESTYLE TRAINER GUIDANCE
============================================================

Add a deterministic read-only trainer-guidance section based on:

- sleep hours,
- stress,
- recovery quality,
- daily activity,
- physical work,
- health limitations / pain text.

Do not diagnose medical conditions.

Examples of practical guidance:

Poor recovery or high stress:
- avoid excessive training to failure,
- prefer 2–3 reps in reserve,
- reduce or avoid intensification methods,
- monitor performance and recovery,
- consider a lighter week when performance and recovery decline.

Low sleep:
- warn that progression and tolerance may be reduced,
- recommend consistent sleep and conservative progression.

Physical work:
- account for additional fatigue, especially for overlapping muscle groups.

Pain or health limitation:
- show a prominent trainer warning,
- suggest pain-free movement variants,
- suggest controlled range of motion and tempo,
- require trainer review before exercise assignment.

For knee-pain text, provide non-diagnostic trainer suggestions such as:

- verify pain location, intensity and aggravating movement,
- avoid exercises and ranges that clearly increase symptoms,
- consider low-load strengthening patterns available in the exercise library,
  such as supported squat patterns, controlled low step-ups or isometric
  quadriceps work,
- recommend specialist consultation for acute injury, swelling, locking,
  instability or worsening symptoms.

Do not automatically add a rehabilitation exercise to a plan.
Show candidates for trainer approval only.

Return structured guidance entries:

- category,
- severity,
- title,
- message,
- trainerAction,
- relatedInputSource.
