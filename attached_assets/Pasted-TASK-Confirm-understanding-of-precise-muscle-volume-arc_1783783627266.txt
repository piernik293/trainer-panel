2. EXERCISE MUSCLE CONTRIBUTIONS
==================================================

Each exercise should support:

- one broad body-part category
- one primary specific muscle
- multiple secondary specific muscles
- an editable volume-contribution coefficient for every assigned muscle

Suggested starting coefficients:

- 1.0 — główny mięsień pracujący
- 0.5 — istotny mięsień pomocniczy
- 0.25 — mniejszy udział pomocniczy
- 0 — mięsień nie jest liczony do objętości

These coefficients are coaching estimates.

They must be editable by the trainer for each exercise.

Do not treat them as scientifically exact or universally fixed values.

The coefficients do not need to add up to 1.0.
