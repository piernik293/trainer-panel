------------------------------------------------

1. BMI calculation

Use existing fields:

- height in cm
- weight in kg

Calculate BMI using:

BMI = weight / (heightInMeters * heightInMeters)

Example:
height 180 cm = 1.8 m

Do not store BMI in the database unless the current architecture already supports derived values.
BMI can be calculated in the frontend and/or trainer panel as a derived value.

------------------------------------------------

2. Show recommendation only for beginners

Show the recommendation only if:

- experience level is "Początkujący"
- height and weight are provided
- calculated BMI is 25 or higher

Do not show this recommendation for:

- Średniozaawansowany
- Zaawansowany

Keep the tone professional, neutral and non-judgmental.

Do not write:

- "Masz nadwagę"

- "Powinieneś schudnąć"

------------------------------------------------

Important:

- Do not automatically select "Redukcja tkanki tłuszczowej"

The recommendation is only a visual suggestion.
