## Goal

Rules:

- unauthenticated request → 401,
- another trainer’s client → 404,
- trainerId comes only from the authenticated Clerk session,
- never trust trainerId from body, query, headers, or URL,
- ownership must be checked before generator services read the survey, history, preferences, or plans,
- do not change generated exercises, volume rules, warnings, or plan structure.
