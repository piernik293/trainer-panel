==================================================
1. PRODUCT RULE
==================================================

For “Brak aktywności fizycznej”, the automatic generator should primarily use
simple developmental exercises that:

- train major movement patterns,
- are easy to explain,
- are easy to scale,
- have stable execution,
- allow controlled loading,
- support general full-body development,
- are appropriate for learning basic movement patterns.

The main weekly patterns remain:

1. knee-dominant,
2. hip-dominant,
3. upper-body push,
4. upper-body pull,
5. core / trunk stability,
6. optional carry or simple accessory.

Do not try to make every small muscle target green.

==================================================
3. EXERCISE PRIORITY
==================================================

Do not globally redesign exercise_priority.

For no-activity clients, selection should prefer:

1. stable bilateral or supported exercises,
2. simple machine/cable exercises,
3. supported bodyweight exercises,
4. controlled free-weight exercises,
5. exercises requiring more balance or coordination.

For this level, safety and suitability must rank above novelty.

Exercise-history novelty may rotate exercises only inside the safe eligible
pool.

An unused but more difficult exercise must not outrank a safer primary exercise
solely because it is new.

Apply the smallest no-activity-specific sorting adjustment only if the current
sort allows this.

Do not change ranking for Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced clients.
