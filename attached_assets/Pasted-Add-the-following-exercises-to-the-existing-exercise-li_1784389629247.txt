Important classification rules:
- High Pull must be classified as an explosive compound movement, not as a simple trapezius isolation exercise.
- Band Pull-Apart should include rear deltoids, middle trapezius and rhomboids.
- External shoulder rotations should primarily target the rotator cuff, especially infraspinatus and teres minor, and should be marked as prehab/accessory exercises.
- Jefferson Curl should be marked as loaded mobility and advanced, not as a standard hypertrophy exercise.
- JM Press should primarily target the triceps, with chest and anterior deltoids as synergists.
- Zercher Squat should primarily target quadriceps, with glutes, adductors, spinal erectors and core as secondary/stabilizing muscles.
- Kroc Row should primarily target the back, with biceps, rear deltoids, forearms, grip and trunk stabilizers as secondary muscles.
